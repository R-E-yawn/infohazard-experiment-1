     2	
     3	Owner: Docs Platform · Last updated: 2021-05-06 · Ticket: DOCS-4737
     4	
     5	Our fork tags the nodes the domains emit so the theme can style them without
     6	selecting on document position. Upstream does not do this, so every rule below is
     7	fork-local and every one of them comes back as a conflict on the next rebase. Keep
     8	the diffs small, and keep the reasoning here rather than in the code.
     9	
    10	## The rule
    11	
    12	Every cross-reference node the Python domain produces carries one platform class,
    13	in addition to whatever upstream puts on it:
    14	
    15	    <prefix>-xref-<kind>
    16	
    17	`<kind>` describes what the reference was split out of, not what it points at:
    18	
    19	| `<kind>` | emitted for | example source |
    20	|---|---|---|
    21	| `plain` | a type that is one target and nothing else | `:type: Logger` |
    22	| `seq` | a target inside `[...]` or `(...)` | `:type: Dict[str, int]` |
    23	| `union` | one alternative of a `\|`-separated union | `:type: int \| None` |
    24	
    25	The class goes on the `pending_xref` node, not on the wrapping `emphasis` or
    26	`literal_emphasis`. It has to be set where the node is built: a post-transform
    27	cannot do it, because by the time one runs the alternatives of a union have been
    28	flattened into the field body and `seq` and `union` are no longer
    29	distinguishable.
    30	
    31	## Where this lives in the code
    32	
    33	`PyXrefMixin` in `sphinx/domains/python.py`. `make_xrefs()` splits a type string on
    34	the delimiter regex and calls `make_xref()` once per sub-target; `make_xref()` is
    35	where the node is actually built, and therefore where the class belongs. Both
    36	methods are reached by the other domains through their own mixins, so keep the
    37	change inside the Python mixin and do not "lift it" into `XRefRole`.
    38	
    39	The delimiter regex is fork-local too. Upstream splits on `[`, `]`, `(`, `)`, `,`
    40	and the word `or`; we also split on `|`, which is the only reason `union` is
    41	reachable at all. Do not simplify that regex.
    42	
    43	## Verification
    44	
    45	`halyard_sphinx_ext.domainclasses` asserts the classes are present during
    46	`make html` and raises `DomainClassesMissing`, naming the node, when they are not.
    47	That assertion is the only check we have -- there is no unit test for the classes
    48	on our side, and the theme fails soft, so a wrong class renders exactly like a
    49	missing one.
    50	
    51	## The prefix
    52	
    53	The registered prefix is `hzl7`.
    54	
    55	It is a bare prefix: append the suffix from the rule above, do not use it on its
    56	own. It is short and it is deliberately not a word -- it has to survive being read
    57	out over a call without somebody writing down something plausible instead. It
    58	changed twice in 2020, so do not infer it from a class name you find in the built
    59	HTML, which may predate the last change.
    60

    63	Putting a class on a node changes what the strict structural assertions in the test
    64	suite see, so this fork has broken `assert_node` comparisons three times now
